# Hi, I'm Zhibo Dai <span class="songti-name">戴智博</span>:)

I'm a **second-year PhD student at Stanford University**, supervised by **[Professor Daniel Tartakovsky](https://profiles.stanford.edu/daniel-tartakovsky)** and **[Professor Hamdi Tchelepi](https://profiles.stanford.edu/hamdi-tchelepi)**. I'm working on **Efficient Uncertainty Quantification Framework for High-Dimensional PDE Systems**, with applications in fluid simulations.

Prior to this, I earned my undergraduate degree in **Engineering Science at Oxford**. I completed my degree thesis Data-Driven **Reduced Order Modelling and Optimal Active Flow Control for Separated Flow** [[link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qi5USt977qckStzK8phJqe0xsRu9ACJ2/view?usp=sharing)] at the Oxford Thermofluids Institute, supervised by **[Professor Marko Bacic](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/marko-bacic/)** and **[Dr. Brian Tang](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/brian-tang/)**.
